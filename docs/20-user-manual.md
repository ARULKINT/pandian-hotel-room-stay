# 20. User Manual (Guest Guide)

This guide explains how to use the Pandian Hotel & Room Stay website as a guest — no technical knowledge required.

## 20.1 How to Start

Open the hotel's website link in your phone or computer's browser. You'll land on the **Home** page, which shows a welcome banner and a quick way to check availability.

## 20.2 How to Check Availability and Browse Rooms

1. On the Home page, tap the **Dates** or **Guests** box to change your check-in date, check-out date, or number of guests.
2. Tap **Check Availability**.
3. You'll see a list of rooms that are free for your dates, with their price per night, size, and a short description.
4. Use the **All Rooms / AC / Non-AC** buttons to narrow the list, or the **Sort** menu to order by price or rating.

## 20.3 How to View a Room's Full Details

Tap **Room Details** on any room card (or tap the room's photo) to see:
- A photo gallery (swipe left/right to see more photos)
- All the amenities included (AC, WiFi, breakfast, etc.)
- The full price, including a "Taxes info" button that shows exactly how much government tax (GST) applies
- Check-in/check-out times, cancellation policy, and house rules

## 20.4 How to Compare Rooms

1. On the Rooms list, tick the **Compare** checkbox on 2 or 3 rooms you're deciding between.
2. A bar appears at the bottom — tap it to see a side-by-side table of prices, sizes, and amenities.
3. You can book directly from this comparison, or remove a room and go back to browsing.

## 20.5 How to Book a Room

1. From any room, tap **Book Now**.
2. **Step 2 — Your Details:**
   - Optionally add extras: airport/station pickup, an Ayurveda massage, a welcome snack, breakfast, or an extra mattress.
   - Fill in your full name, email address, and mobile number (all required).
   - Add any special requests (e.g. "early check-in" or "less spicy food") if you like.
   - Check the running total at the bottom, then tap **Continue**.
3. **Step 3 — Payment:**
   - Choose how you'd like to pay: UPI, Card, or Pay at Hotel.
   - Review the full bill (room, extras, discount, tax).
   - Tap **Confirm Booking**.
4. You'll land on a **Booking Confirmed** page with your Booking ID — save or screenshot this. You can tap the copy icon next to it to copy the ID.

> **Note**: This is a demo/booking-management system — no real payment is charged regardless of which payment method you choose. In a live hotel deployment, "Pay at Hotel" simply means you settle the bill in person at check-in.

## 20.6 How to Find or Cancel a Booking Later

1. Tap **My Stays** in the bottom navigation bar (the calendar icon).
2. Enter the same email address you used when booking, and tap **Find**.
3. Your upcoming and past stays will appear.
4. To cancel an upcoming booking, tap **Cancel** on that booking card and confirm. Cancellation is free up to 48 hours before your check-in date.

## 20.7 How to Browse Hotel Facilities

Tap **Facilities** in the bottom navigation bar to see everything the hotel offers — restaurant, filter coffee corner, Ayurveda massage, parking, front desk, and laundry — each with timings and location.

## 20.8 Common Problems

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| The room I want shows "Sold Out" | Someone else has already booked all the remaining rooms of that type for your dates. Try different dates or a different room. |
| I don't see my booking under "My Stays" | Double-check you're entering the exact email address you used when booking. |
| The page shows "Cannot reach the server" | Check your internet connection. If it keeps happening, the hotel's booking system may be temporarily down — try again in a minute (on the free hosting plan, the very first visit after a period of inactivity can take up to a minute to load). |
| I want to change my dates after booking | There's no "edit booking" option — cancel the existing booking (if outside the free-cancellation window, a one-night charge applies as hotel credit) and make a new one. |

## 20.9 Frequently Asked Questions

**Do I need to create an account?**
No. You only need an email address, which is used to look up your booking later — there's no password or sign-up.

**Is my payment actually processed?**
No — this version of the site does not connect to a real payment provider. The payment step is for demonstration/booking-management purposes only.

**Will I get an email confirmation?**
The confirmation screen states that details were sent to your email, but no email is actually sent by this version of the system — please save your Booking ID from the confirmation screen instead.

**Can I book for someone else?**
Yes — just enter the guest's name and their own contact details (or yours, if you'll be the point of contact) at the details step.
